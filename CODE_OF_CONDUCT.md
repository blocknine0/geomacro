# Geomacro Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Geomacro is committed to maintaining a professional, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who interacts with the project.

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in Geomacro-managed spaces, including GitHub issues, discussions, security reports, technical communications, and other official project channels.

## Expected Conduct

Participants are expected to:

- communicate professionally and respectfully;
- engage in technical disagreements constructively;
- provide relevant and good-faith feedback;
- respect the privacy and security of others;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks;
- avoid publishing another person's private or identifying information without permission;
- avoid deliberately disrupting project discussions, issue tracking, or security processes;
- follow responsible disclosure practices when reporting security vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behavior is not acceptable in Geomacro-managed spaces:

- harassment, threats, or discriminatory abuse;
- personal attacks or deliberately hostile communication;
- publication of private information without authorization;
- spam, malicious submissions, or deliberate disruption;
- attempts to use project communication channels for fraud, impersonation, or deceptive activity;
- irresponsible disclosure of security vulnerabilities that may put users, infrastructure, funds, or project systems at risk.

## Technical Disagreements

Technical disagreement is expected and can improve the project.

Participants should focus discussion on evidence, implementation, architecture, security, and measurable trade-offs rather than individuals.

Maintainers may close or moderate discussions that become abusive, repetitive, intentionally disruptive, or unrelated to the project.

## Enforcement

Geomacro maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, contributions, or other interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the circumstances, participants may receive a warning or may be temporarily or permanently restricted from participating in project-managed spaces.

## Security Reports

Security vulnerabilities should not be disclosed publicly when doing so could expose users, infrastructure, funds, credentials, or protocol operations to unnecessary risk.

Please follow the reporting process described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
