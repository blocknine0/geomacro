# Geomacro Global Realtime Intelligence 100% Gate

This is the canonical production gate for the realtime intelligence foundation.

100% means 100% of the governed target universe is represented, recently observed within product freshness rules, independently observable where required, commercially certified where required, and connected to the realtime orchestration path.

It does not mean every real-world event on Earth can be observed.

## Serial acceptance

1. Canonical 195-country universe: exactly 195 enabled countries and 585 country/category cells.
2. Raw source matrix: every country/category cell has its governed target topology.
3. Fresh raw runtime: all 195 x 3 cells have a recent successful raw acquisition.
4. Non-GDELT runtime independence: all 585 cells have at least one fresh non-GDELT target.
5. GDELT first-break: gdelt_gal/global-relevant is healthy and no more than 1,800 seconds old.
6. Corridor and hot-topic fanout: every governed corridor and required shock/hot-topic family has all three category burst paths and the scope contract is green.
7. Direct operational sources: every enabled WEB_DIRECT target is fresh and error-free. Partial health is not enough for 100%.
8. Source certification: every required source is certified across REGISTRY, ENDPOINT, RIGHTS, SCHEMA, FRESHNESS, PROVENANCE, INDEPENDENCE, ADAPTER, RUNTIME and FALLBACK, with every required queue path green.
9. Realtime orchestrator continuity: core realtime tasks have recent healthy successful heartbeats with zero consecutive failures.

## Hard rule

Any failed serial gate is RED. The exact failed country, category, source, target, task or certification path is preserved in the gate artifact.

No freshness threshold, rights rule, source requirement or corroboration rule may be silently weakened to turn the result green.

## Commercial boundary

This gate is read-only. It does not enable payments, promote sources, grant licences, publish a Risk Object, settle USDC or authorize execution.

A green result is the prerequisite foundation for the later pay-per-call acceptance gates. It is not itself payment authorization.

## Owner evidence

The owner may need to complete source-specific licence/reuse evidence, provider approvals or credentials and run the production workflow against the authoritative Supabase project. Those are evidence inputs, not reasons to bypass a failed gate.
