# Technical Proof Positioning

When presenting Geomacro externally, technical proof should support the intelligence product rather than replace it.

Primary story: explainable geopolitical and macro risk intelligence for human and machine decisions.

Supporting proof may include Arc Testnet, USDC, Circle x402, signed Risk Objects, Risk Gate machine delivery and the experimental prediction-market application. These demonstrate integration and programmable-delivery capability. They do not make Geomacro a prediction-market company, a wallet custodian or an autonomous execution provider.
