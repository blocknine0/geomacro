# Founding Pilot Pricing Guardrails

Internal founder-use only. Do not publish these numbers as general pricing until Geomacro has enough paid-pilot evidence to support a stable public plan.

## Objective

The first commercial goal is to obtain a real paid validation signal without pricing Geomacro like a hobby project or pretending it already has enterprise-scale support.

The buyer should pay for access to a defined decision-support workflow, evidence review, and founder-supported validation. The buyer is not buying autonomous execution, custody, an SLA, or unlimited custom development.

## Default founding offers

### A. Analyst / operational workflow pilot

**Default quote: USD 1,500 for 30 days**

Use when the pilot is primarily human-reviewed and covers one operational workflow with up to two countries or one directional corridor.

Includes:

- current intelligence and GRI where relevant;
- evidence / confidence / freshness review;
- one founder onboarding session;
- weekly review;
- agreed exports or documented outputs that already exist in the product;
- final pilot evidence and continuation review.

### B. Risk API / Risk Gate technical pilot

**Default quote: USD 2,500 for 30 days**

Use when the buyer needs machine-readable Risk Objects, controlled API access, Risk Gate evaluation, or engineering support to test a real integration.

Includes the analyst-pilot scope plus:

- controlled Private Pilot API path;
- signed Risk Object delivery where applicable;
- Risk Gate integration support;
- reasonable pilot request volume agreed before start;
- one technical integration session;
- final integration evidence review.

### C. Strategic design partner

**Quote individually. Do not default to free.**

A discount can be justified only when the partner supplies unusual strategic value, for example:

- credible reference-customer potential;
- meaningful proprietary workflow feedback;
- distribution;
- technical infrastructure Geomacro materially needs;
- strategic investment or acquisition dialogue;
- access to regulated or institutional design requirements that would otherwise be difficult to obtain.

A free pilot should be the exception. If there is no commercial commitment, keep the engagement to a short demo or structured design interview rather than open-ended custom work.

## Discount floor

Do not reduce the 30-day paid pilot below **USD 750** without a written strategic reason.

Any discount should buy something concrete, such as:

- a defined reference right subject to buyer approval;
- structured weekly feedback;
- access to the integration team;
- a clear continuation decision date;
- a strategic introduction;
- distribution or design-partner value.

Do not discount simply because Geomacro is early-stage.

## Scope expansion

Increase the quote when the buyer asks for materially more than the default pilot, including:

- additional countries or corridors;
- custom integration work;
- high request volume;
- additional user groups;
- custom reporting cadence;
- bespoke research;
- source licensing or data acquisition costs;
- security / procurement work beyond the normal pilot package.

Scope increases should be priced before work starts, not absorbed silently.

## Post-pilot recurring anchors

These are negotiation anchors, not public plans and not promises of current general availability.

### Professional intelligence continuation

**Target anchor: USD 750 to 1,250 per month**

For a narrow recurring human-reviewed workflow using current intelligence, GRI, research, monitoring and agreed exports.

### API / Risk Gate continuation

**Target anchor: USD 1,500 to 3,000 per month**

For a narrow production-oriented machine workflow after source eligibility, reliability, rate limits, support expectations and deployment boundaries have been validated.

### Wider institutional agreement

**Start discussion around USD 24,000 to 36,000 annual contract value** for a genuinely broader institutional deployment, then price upward based on users, workflows, geography, API volume, support, data rights and procurement requirements.

Do not offer an enterprise SLA or production support obligation until Geomacro has the operational capacity to meet it.

## Payment and contract principle

For early pilots, prefer:

- fixed scope;
- fixed 30-day term;
- payment before or at pilot start where commercially practical;
- no auto-renewal by default;
- written limitations and success criteria;
- a scheduled go / no-go review before the pilot ends.

Do not tie institutional pricing to the Arc Testnet x402 demonstration price. The `0.001 USDC` testnet price exists only to prove machine payment and delivery mechanics.

## Negotiation rule

Never lead with price before the workflow is understood.

Sequence:

`buyer problem -> decision -> scope -> measurable value -> pilot design -> price`

If the buyer cannot describe a real decision or cannot identify who would own continuation, do not perform unpaid custom engineering merely to keep the conversation alive.
