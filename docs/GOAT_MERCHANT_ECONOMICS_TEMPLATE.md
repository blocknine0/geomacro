## Why x402 is commercially useful

- autonomous software can purchase a single intelligence unit without a manual subscription workflow;
- pricing can be metered per machine request;
- payment state can bind directly to one request/resource/delivery record;
- the merchant rail can support repeat agent usage while Geomacro keeps broader enterprise billing separate;
- the integration produces traceable request/order/resource evidence useful for machine-to-machine commerce.

## What not to claim

- Testnet calls are not revenue;
- Testnet USDC or test transactions are not commercial volume;
- Mainnet chain/token/limits are not inferred from Testnet3;
- no design partner or paying customer is claimed until confirmed;
- no revenue forecast should be presented without explicit assumptions.
