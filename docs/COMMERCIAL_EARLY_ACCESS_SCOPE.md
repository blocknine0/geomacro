# Commercial Early Access Scope

Geomacro's first commercial Early Access should be a narrow founding-pilot engagement, not a claim of broad enterprise availability.

## Offer

One real operational workflow using Geomacro risk intelligence, such as:

- a country-risk review;
- a directional corridor review;
- a treasury or cross-border payment pre-flight;
- an exposure-monitoring workflow for a risk or strategy team;
- an agent/software workflow that needs signed external risk context before a customer-controlled action.

## Pilot inputs

Agree in advance on:

- customer/user role;
- country or directional corridor scope;
- decision being supported;
- expected evidence and freshness needs;
- desired human or machine-readable output;
- review cadence;
- success criteria;
- exclusions and limitations.

## Product boundary

The pilot may use public intelligence, GRI, research and a controlled Risk Gate / Risk API path. It must not imply:

- autonomous execution by Geomacro;
- wallet custody or transaction signing;
- full route/logistics/counterparty modelling where only endpoint-composed corridor risk exists;
- sanctions-screening replacement;
- an enterprise SLA before one is actually offered;
- third-party security or methodology certification that has not been obtained.

The current Risk Gate execution boundary remains `execution_authorized=false`.

## Technical proof

Circle x402, USDC, Arc Testnet and the prediction-market application may be shown when relevant to a technical buyer. They demonstrate integration capability and agent-native access patterns; they are not the core product identity or the basis of institutional pricing.

## Commercial close

A pilot should end with a documented go/no-go decision based on usefulness, evidence quality, workflow fit, integration effort and buyer willingness to continue. Wider packaging, pricing and deployment should follow validated pilot evidence rather than precede it.
