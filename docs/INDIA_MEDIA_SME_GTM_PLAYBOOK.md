# India Media + SME GTM Playbook

Status: founder-use commercial playbook.
Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

Objective: win the first Indian paid customers with narrow, measurable risk-intelligence workflows that do not require bank-scale procurement.

## ICP 1 — Media / business-news / research

Strong fit signals:
- covers geopolitics, macroeconomics, trade, energy, supply chains or international business;
- analysts repeatedly assemble country context manually from multiple sources;
- needs evidence-backed context quickly when events move;
- publishes or briefs frequently enough that monitoring has recurring value;
- has an editor, research lead or commercial owner who can approve a paid evaluation.

Weak fit signals:
- wants free article-writing labor rather than a risk-intelligence tool;
- requires unrestricted raw-content redistribution;
- expects Geomacro to replace editorial verification/judgment;
- has no recurring country/macro research workflow.

Best first offer:
- Media & Research Starter Evaluation;
- 30 days;
- 3 to 5 agreed countries/themes;
- use existing product surfaces and governed derived output;
- no bespoke integration.

Discovery questions:
1. Which countries or macro themes take the most research time each week?
2. When a story breaks, what evidence must your team confirm before publishing?
3. Which changes are easy to miss until they become a headline?
4. Would a structured risk/change feed be more useful than another news feed?
5. Who decides whether the tool becomes a recurring research expense after 30 days?

Demo sequence:
1. open one current country/event the buyer already understands;
2. show risk/change, evidence, confidence and freshness;
3. show what changed and what cannot be verified;
4. show one additional country/theme from their coverage universe;
5. close on a 30-day evaluation, not a broad partnership.

Success evidence:
- research minutes/hours saved;
- useful-signal rate;
- number of stories/briefs where the intelligence materially helped;
- evidence traceability satisfaction;
- conversion decision.

## ICP 2 — Small/mid import-export and manufacturing

Strong fit signals:
- supplier/customer exposure across multiple countries;
- procurement or sales decisions affected by conflict, policy, macro or currency conditions;
- currently relies on manual news/WhatsApp/search updates;
- has a clear owner in procurement, finance, operations, strategy or founder office;
- can name the top 5 to 10 countries that matter.

Best first offer:
- SME Country Risk Monitor Pilot;
- one use case;
- up to 10 countries;
- human-reviewed workflow first;
- Risk Gate only for currently supported country/action profiles.

Discovery questions:
1. Which foreign countries can disrupt revenue, procurement or delivery?
2. What decision changes when risk rises?
3. How do you monitor those countries today?
4. What is the cost of learning about a material change late?
5. Who reviews the signal and what action can they take?

Best initial workflows:
- supplier-country review;
- export-market exposure review;
- market-entry monitoring;
- strategic-input country monitoring;
- weekly management risk brief.

Do not initially sell:
- vessel routing;
- port-operation prediction;
- counterparty screening;
- sanctions clearance;
- transaction approval;
unless those exact modules/workflows are separately production-ready.

## ICP 3 — Logistics / freight / commodity businesses

Strong fit signals:
- materially exposed to geopolitical or macro changes across origin/destination countries;
- monitors shipping/commodity context manually;
- can benefit from country-level early warning even before route-level Risk Gate modules are complete.

Sell today:
- country exposure monitoring;
- evidence-backed event/change context;
- country-level risk review where readiness passes;
- recurring briefs.

Do not imply today:
- full port/chokepoint/vessel/route intelligence;
- insurance pricing;
- real-time freight disruption prediction.

## ICP 4 — Boutique consulting / advisory

Strong fit signals:
- serves clients with country/macro exposure;
- analyst time is expensive;
- wants structured evidence and change context to support its own judgment;
- can use Geomacro as an internal analyst tool or governed data input.

Commercial boundary:
- do not grant white-label or redistribution rights by default;
- define client-facing use explicitly in the SOW;
- consulting judgment remains with the buyer.

## ICP 5 — Fintech / treasury-tech / enterprise AI

Use only when the prospect has a concrete machine workflow.

Best first offer:
- Risk API + Risk Gate Technical Pilot.

Before proposal:
- run exact request-readiness check;
- confirm all activated modules are production-supported;
- confirm countries have current readiness evidence;
- define request volume and credentials;
- confirm no unsupported execution claim.

If the desired action profile activates unsupported modules, sell design discovery or a narrower supported workflow instead of pretending the requested integration is ready.

## First-contact rule

Lead with the buyer's decision problem, not Geomacro architecture.

Preferred structure:

`their exposure -> what changes -> evidence gap -> short Geomacro proof -> 30-day measurable evaluation`

Do not lead with:
- prediction markets;
- Arc;
- x402;
- smart contracts;
- AI-agent jargon;
unless the prospect is specifically technical and that proof is relevant.

## Qualification score

A prospect is strong when at least 4 of these 5 are true:
- real recurring risk/research problem;
- named countries/exposures;
- identifiable decision owner;
- measurable value/success criterion;
- commercial owner/budget path.

If fewer than 3 are true, keep it to a short demo/discovery call rather than custom pilot work.

## Proof before proposal

Before sending a proposal, capture:
- buyer/use case;
- requested countries;
- exact output needed;
- country readiness result;
- action-profile readiness result if Risk Gate/API is involved;
- source-rights boundary;
- relevant security/readiness evidence;
- pilot tier and price;
- measurable success criteria.

No proposal should contain a static global-support claim detached from current evidence.

## Conversion path

Starter Evaluation -> Professional Risk Pilot when:
- the buyer wants more countries/themes;
- analyst workflow becomes recurring;
- custom exports/reports are requested;
- founder support becomes materially deeper.

SME Pilot -> recurring intelligence when:
- the monitored exposures remain active;
- buyer can show decision value;
- support/volume is sustainable.

Human workflow -> API/Risk Gate only when:
- machine delivery adds measurable value;
- exact action profile is supported;
- integration owner exists;
- security/access boundaries are ready.

## Founder operating discipline

For every active prospect maintain:
- stage;
- ICP;
- champion;
- problem;
- countries/exposures;
- proposed offer;
- quoted price;
- proof sent;
- objections;
- next decision/date;
- conversion probability;
- reason if lost.

Do not customize the core product for a prospect before a real commercial signal unless the work also closes a permanent Geomacro roadmap gap.
