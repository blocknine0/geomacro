# Geomacro India B2B Package

Status: permanent commercial standard for India until revised.

Geomacro provides explainable geopolitical and macro risk intelligence for human and machine decisions. The commercial product is Risk Intelligence, governed Data/API, signed Risk Objects and Risk Gate. Prediction-market and onchain work remain supporting technical proof.

This package is for controlled paid pilots and enterprise diligence. It does not represent regulator approval, a security certification, or a general production SLA.

## Current paid offers

- Professional Risk Intelligence Pilot: INR 125,000 for 30 days.
- Risk API + Risk Gate Technical Pilot: INR 210,000 for 30 days.
- Institutional deployment: custom scope after diligence.
- Founder-led custom risk brief: custom scope.

Default paid-pilot discount floor: INR 65,000 unless a written strategic reason justifies a lower price.

- current geopolitical and macro risk context;
- evidence, confidence, freshness and change context;
- governed country structural profiles where source rights permit;
- authenticated machine-readable API access;

- directional country-pair context under the explicitly labelled pilot methodology;
- structured exports where implemented and contractually scoped;

## Hard boundaries

- Geomacro does not authorize customer transactions or hold customer funds.
- `execution_authorized=false` remains the Risk Gate boundary.
- Geomacro is not a substitute for customer compliance, sanctions, AML/KYC, legal or regulated approval processes.
- Current corridor methodology is endpoint-composed and is not full route, vessel, counterparty or correspondent-bank modelling.
- Only commercially eligible data may enter paid customer output.
- Missing, review-gated or ineligible data fails closed.
- No SOC 2, ISO 27001, regulator approval, external penetration-test certification or production SLA may be claimed unless actually obtained and current.
