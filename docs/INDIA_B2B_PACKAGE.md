# Geomacro India B2B Commercial Package

Status: permanent India commercial standard until revised.
Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

Geomacro provides explainable geopolitical and macro risk intelligence for human and machine decisions. The commercial product is Risk Intelligence, governed Data/API, signed Risk Objects and Risk Gate. Prediction-market and onchain work remain supporting technical proof rather than the primary buyer proposition.

This document localizes scope and quoting for India. `docs/FOUNDING_PILOT_PRICING_GUARDRAILS.md` remains the canonical global pricing guardrail for full founding pilots. If an INR quote conflicts with the canonical USD discount floor at quote time, the canonical floor wins unless a written strategic exception is approved.

## Go-to-market order

India is not bank-first.

Primary early buyers:

1. media, business-news and research teams;
2. small and mid-sized import/export, manufacturing, logistics, commodities and supply-chain companies;
3. boutique consulting, advisory and research firms;
4. fintech, treasury-tech, payments and enterprise-AI teams that need API or machine-readable risk context;
5. larger banks, NBFCs, insurers, capital-markets firms and regulated enterprises after the required procurement/security pack is ready.

## Pricing and discount control

Normal rule:
- do not discount simply because Geomacro is early-stage;
- full founding pilots follow `docs/FOUNDING_PILOT_PRICING_GUARDRAILS.md`;
- the canonical full-pilot discount floor is USD 750 unless a written strategic reason is recorded;
- the INR figures above are India quoting anchors, not a mechanism to bypass the USD floor;
- the INR 35,000 Media & Research Starter Evaluation is allowed only because its scope is materially narrower than a founding pilot and excludes custom engineering/API/Risk Gate integration;
- if a starter evaluation expands into custom research, integration, API/Risk Gate work or materially broader support, re-scope and re-price it before the work starts.

Strategic discounts should buy something concrete, such as reference-customer value, structured feedback, distribution, integration access, a continuation decision date or another documented strategic benefit.

## What Geomacro may commercially provide

Subject to current production evidence and source rights:

- geopolitical and macro country risk context;
- risk score/change context where the methodology is production-approved;
- evidence/provenance, confidence and freshness;
- signed/verifiable Risk Objects;
- governed API access;
- supported Risk Gate policy evaluation;
- structured exports;
- selected-country monitoring;
- founder-led risk briefs and pilot reviews;
- historical/change-attribution tools where the data/methodology is production-ready.

## What must not be promised

Until separately implemented and evidenced:

- complete sanctions screening;
- KYC/AML replacement;
- legal or regulatory approval;
- transaction authorization;
- full route/vessel/port/correspondent-bank modelling;
- all-country support merely because a country exists in the registry;
- all Risk Gate action classes merely because country review is supported;
- real-time or uptime SLA unless contracted and measured;
- SOC 2, ISO 27001, CERT-In certification, RBI/SEBI/IRDAI approval or equivalent certification;
- third-party raw-data redistribution rights not explicitly held.

## Country support rule

A country is sellable for a Risk Gate country-review workflow only when current evidence proves all required production modules are available and commercially verified for that country.

The permanent evidence source is the Global Risk Gate Country Readiness census.

If a required module, source-rights state, freshness check or verification check is missing, the country/workflow fails closed. Sales and proposals must not override this rule.

## Action support rule

Country support and workflow support are separate gates.

Before offering any API/Risk Gate workflow, run the request-readiness check for the exact action shape. If any activated module is not production-supported, the workflow remains pilot-blocked/fail-closed until that module is promoted through the governed support process.

## Pilot success metrics

Every paid pilot must define measurable outcomes before kickoff. Choose the smallest relevant set:

- percentage of requested country/exposure checks successfully served;
- evidence freshness and coverage rate;
- analyst/research time saved;
- useful-signal rate;
- false-positive/false-block rate where measurable;
- number of decisions/research outputs where Geomacro materially added context;
- API integration success rate/latency for technical pilots;
- buyer willingness to continue, expand or convert to recurring usage.

## Standard onboarding flow

1. qualify buyer and use case;
2. confirm countries/exposures and exact workflow;
3. run country/action readiness check;
4. confirm source-rights and output-delivery boundary;
5. agree SOW, price, payment terms and success metrics;
6. provision bounded access;
7. complete kickoff and baseline measurement;
8. run weekly review;
9. deliver pilot-end evidence/ROI report;
10. convert, expand, pause or stop based on measured outcome.
