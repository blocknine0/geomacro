# Geomacro India B2B Package

Status: permanent commercial standard for India until revised.
Last reviewed: 2026-09-14.

Geomacro provides explainable geopolitical and macro risk intelligence for human and machine decisions. The commercial product is Risk Intelligence, governed Data/API, signed Risk Objects and Risk Gate. Prediction-market and onchain work remain supporting technical proof.

This package is designed to win the first Indian B2B revenue from buyers with a real external-risk workflow and relatively low procurement friction, while preserving a credible path to larger regulated institutions later.

It does not represent regulator approval, a security certification, investment advice, sanctions clearance, or a general production SLA.

## Go-to-market order

### Tier 1: first-revenue buyers

Prioritize these before large banks:

1. business, finance and geopolitical media teams;
2. independent research and intelligence firms;
3. export/import SMEs and mid-market companies;
4. manufacturing and supply-chain teams;
5. logistics, freight and trade-services companies;
6. commodities, energy and critical-minerals businesses;
7. boutique consulting and advisory firms;
8. fintech, treasury-software and enterprise-AI startups that need machine-readable risk context.

These buyers can often validate value through a narrow 30-day workflow without the procurement depth required by a bank or large regulated institution.

### Tier 2: larger enterprise buyers

- large exporters/importers;
- enterprise treasury and strategy teams;
- insurers and brokers where scope is appropriate;
- financial-data and market-intelligence companies;
- larger fintech and payments businesses;
- institutional research teams.

### Tier 3: regulated production buyers

- banks;
- NBFCs;
- payments banks;
- SEBI-regulated entities;
- IRDAI-regulated entities;
- other buyers whose procurement requires formal third-party technology/security diligence.

Tier 3 is strategically important, but it is not the first-revenue dependency.

## Permanent commercial offers

### 1. Media & Research Intelligence Pilot

Target buyer: business-news desk, geopolitical desk, research publisher, independent intelligence team, boutique advisory.

Default scope: 30 days.

Deliverables may include:

- monitored countries and themes agreed at onboarding;
- current geopolitical and macro risk state;
- evidence-backed event briefs;
- score/change context where a verified methodology is available;
- source/provenance and confidence context;
- scheduled founder-led review;
- governed CSV/JSON export when implemented and contractually scoped;
- API access only if explicitly included.

Default founding price: INR 75,000 for 30 days.

This is the lowest-friction paid entry product. It is not a newsroom content syndication licence for third-party source material. Customer publication rights apply only to Geomacro-owned derived output and the exact contracted fields.

### 2. SME External Risk Pilot

Target buyer: exporter/importer, manufacturer, supply-chain team, logistics company, commodity business, internationally exposed SME or mid-market company.

Default scope: 30 days.

Deliverables may include:

- agreed country exposure watchlist;
- current risk and change monitoring;
- evidence/confidence/freshness context;
- country structural profile where commercially eligible;
- decision briefs for sourcing, market exposure or operational review;
- founder-led weekly review;
- explicit unavailable/insufficient-data states instead of fabricated low-risk values.

Default founding price: INR 95,000 for 30 days.

### 3. Professional Risk Intelligence Pilot

Target buyer: professional research, risk, strategy, treasury or institutional analyst team.

Default scope: 30 days.

Default founding price: INR 125,000 for 30 days.

Includes deeper analytics, historical/change attribution where available, governed country/corridor profile views and agreed structured exports.

### 4. Risk API + Risk Gate Technical Pilot

Target buyer: fintech, treasury platform, enterprise-AI system, agent platform, payments infrastructure, financial software provider.

Default scope: 30 days.

Default founding price: INR 210,000 for 30 days.

Deliverables may include:

- authenticated governed API entitlement;
- machine-readable country risk responses for currently supported subjects;
- signed Risk Objects;
- public-key verification;
- Risk Gate policy evaluation;
- deterministic fail-closed behavior;
- agreed integration support;
- bounded request volume and subject scope.

The customer always owns downstream execution. Geomacro keeps `execution_authorized=false`.

### 5. Contracted Enterprise Deployment

Custom scope and price after diligence.

Only implemented and evidenced controls may be contracted. SLA, SSO, tenant isolation, dedicated infrastructure, retention, support and audit commitments are negotiated only when the underlying capability exists and has evidence.

## Global country coverage rule

Geomacro must not market a static number of supported countries unless that number comes from a current automated acceptance report.

Every country belongs to an explicit readiness state, for example:

- `ACCEPTED`: current governed data is sufficient, commercial eligibility passes, the signed artifact verifies, and the supported Risk Gate path passes;
- `LIMITED`: some governed Risk Gate v2 modules are available but the system cannot claim the full target context;
- `INSUFFICIENT`: available evidence does not support a normal commercial decision response;
- `BLOCKED_SOURCE_RIGHTS`: useful evidence exists but commercial delivery is not permitted under the current source policy;
- `BLOCKED_DATA_COVERAGE`: required current inputs are missing or stale;
- `ERROR`: an operational acceptance check failed.

Quiet countries must never be converted to zero risk merely because no current event was observed.

The current accepted-country count, denominator, methodology version, run timestamp and failure reasons must be reproducible from stored evidence.

## Media-specific boundary

Geomacro sells derived intelligence, not third-party article republication.

A media customer may receive the Geomacro-created risk state, attribution, confidence, structured facts and permitted provenance according to the contract. Raw provider payloads, publisher article bodies, images, paywalled text or other restricted source material are not redistributed unless separately licensed.

## SME-specific boundary

Geomacro provides external-risk context for business decisions. It does not guarantee shipment performance, supplier solvency, legal compliance, sanctions clearance, insurance coverage, creditworthiness or transaction success.

Customer teams remain responsible for their commercial, legal, compliance and operational decisions.

## Hard product boundaries

- Geomacro does not authorize customer transactions or hold customer funds.
- `execution_authorized=false` remains the Risk Gate boundary.
- Geomacro is not a substitute for sanctions screening, AML/KYC, legal review, regulated approval or investment advice.
- Current corridor methodology is endpoint-composed and is not full route, vessel, counterparty or correspondent-bank modelling.
- Only commercially eligible data may enter paid customer output.
- Missing, review-gated or ineligible data fails closed.
- No SOC 2, ISO 27001, regulator approval, external penetration-test certification or production SLA may be claimed unless actually obtained and current.

## Pilot onboarding minimum

Every paid pilot must record:

1. buyer and team;
2. business decision being supported;
3. countries/corridors/themes in scope;
4. required output format;
5. cadence;
6. customer data, if any;
7. named users or API client;
8. commercial price and payment terms;
9. start/end date;
10. success metrics;
11. explicit exclusions;
12. source-rights/product limitations;
13. escalation contact;
14. stop/renewal decision date.

## Pilot success metrics

Choose a small measurable set, such as:

- analyst research time reduced;
- material external-risk changes detected before the customer's existing workflow;
- percentage of delivered alerts judged relevant;
- API/Risk Gate integration success rate;
- number of customer decisions where Geomacro supplied useful external context;
- false escalation / missed-material-event review;
- evidence/provenance completeness;
- user adoption during the pilot.

Do not claim ROI without customer evidence.
