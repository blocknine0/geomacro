# Existing-news-only commercial structured output — launch constraint

## Owner decision (2026-10-30)

GeoMacro will use news already obtained through its existing three-category discovery/ingestion system to generate **GeoMacro-authored, question-specific structured outputs** for users and machines. **Do not purchase or integrate paid news feeds/providers** for this launch. Do not add new data vendors or change pricing.

Categories: Geopolitics; Macro/FX; Critical Minerals.

For each answer, supply these five sections when supported by eligible evidence:
1. What happened: event-specific verified facts.
2. Why it matters: analysis specific to that event, not repeated generic text.
3. Market relevance: supported trading/FX/commodity implications; otherwise clearly say not established.
4. What to watch: verified next developments, or explicit uncertainty.
5. Evidence and confidence: source-native original publication time, verification status, evidence gaps and confidence.

## Mandatory existing controls — not an authorization bypass

An article being discoverable, ingested, archived, or available on a free website **does not itself grant commercial derived-use permission**. The existing commercial source/rights/certification gates remain authoritative. Use only evidence that passes those gates for a chargeable answer. Keep pending/unknown-rights evidence in the uncharged research/observational path until independently approved; never silently label it commercial-eligible.

Retain existing canonical priced variants, availability-before-402, immutable exact-answer preparation, settlement checks, fail-closed behavior, and output source-disclosure restrictions. No raw article redistribution or invented evidence, timestamps, scores, effects or signatures.

GeoMacro-authored output must be generated based on the exact question and qualified already-collected news; do not purchase upstream news. Do not charge if the evidence required for the selected capability is absent or ineligible.

## Protected boundaries

Do not change PREDGE code, workflow, quotas, credentials or operating behavior; historical repository/Supabase; existing D1/B2 source of truth; pricing, entitlements, signing authorities or settlement authorization. No mainnet activation or real-USDC payment from this decision.

## Verification before declaring implemented

A separate implementation and production test must prove: selected existing-news records pass commercial rights; each of three categories produces non-generic five-part responses grounded in the exact question; missing or unlicensed evidence fails without charge; exact priced JSON is frozen before a 402; authorized payment returns matching bytes and ledger receipt. This document records the owner product decision only, **not** a production implementation or paid-delivery PASS.
