# Existing-news-only automated structured intelligence — founder scope (2026-10-30)

## Required pipeline — no paid news purchases

Use **all news that GeoMacro already lawfully collects** across Geopolitics, Macro/FX, and Critical Minerals. Keep the incoming articles/observations in the existing **private raw-news layer**, preserving original timestamps, immutable IDs, and provenance. Do not purchase or introduce paid news feeds or new upstream providers.

For each collected news record, **automatically create GeoMacro's own derived structured analysis**. Also resolve user/machine questions against relevant collected news and return a query-specific structured output. No additional manual commercial-approval step is required merely to store raw news internally or generate a draft derived analysis. Use existing ingestion, B2/D1, scheduling, deduplication, and internal evidence handling; do not create parallel storage.

## Five-part output

1. `what_happened`: exact event facts, no generic repeats.
2. `why_it_matters`: event-specific cause/consequence, not a reusable boilerplate.
3. `market_relevance`: evidence-grounded trading, FX, commodity implications, or explicit uncertainty.
4. `what_to_watch`: verified developments or explicit unknowns.
5. `evidence_and_confidence`: original publication clock, factual verification, missing evidence, calibrated confidence.

Apply the same generator to all three categories. A question selects matching news, not automatically every record. Do not invent facts, scores, timelines, impacts, or approvals.

## Commercial delivery (existing controls retained)

Raw private retention, analysis generation, and **permission to sell/distribute an output** are separate operations. Generating GeoMacro-owned expression does not by itself clear third-party copyright, commercial extraction, or source-contract restrictions. Keep the existing source-specific paid-output rights/certification policy at the **chargeable delivery boundary**, not as a blanket obstacle to raw retention or generating internal analysis. When rights are unclear, retain raw records and produce internal/uncharged drafts, but do not represent them as commercially cleared until verified. Do not deliver raw third-party articles as paid products.

Reuse existing paid intelligence capability pricing, availability-before-402, exact response-byte freeze, settlement, ledger, idempotency, and source-disclosure policies. No mainnet activation is authorized by this specification.

## Hard boundaries

PREDGE implementation and operations unchanged. Historical data repository/Supabase unchanged. Existing D1/B2 architecture, pricing, entitlements, signing authority, and x402 settlement activation unchanged.

## Implementation and verification still required

This is a **founder product specification**, not a claim of completed automation. Implement and test the automatic raw-news → derived five-part output transformation across all three categories, event-specific quality, multilingual query matching, preserved original time/provenance, safe commercialization decisions, no-charge failures, and eventual byte-exact paid delivery. The implementation must show real production readbacks before being called launched.
