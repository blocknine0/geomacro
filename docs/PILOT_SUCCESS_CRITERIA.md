# Founding Pilot Success Criteria

A Geomacro founding pilot should be evaluated against explicit evidence rather than broad product impressions.

Minimum review areas:

1. Decision relevance: did the risk output materially help the agreed workflow?
2. Evidence quality: could the buyer trace the result to useful, credible source context?
3. Explainability: were score, confidence, freshness and reason codes understandable?
4. Workflow fit: did the output arrive in a form and cadence the buyer could use?
5. Machine usability, when applicable: were signed Risk Objects and policy results practical to integrate?
6. Boundary clarity: did Geomacro avoid implying custody, execution authorization or unsupported modelling coverage?
7. Reliability: were unavailable or unverifiable inputs disclosed rather than replaced with fabricated values?
8. Commercial intent: does the buyer want to continue into a paid or expanded engagement after reviewing the evidence?

Before a pilot begins, turn these areas into buyer-specific measurable criteria. Do not publish generalized customer or performance claims from an isolated pilot without evidence and permission.
