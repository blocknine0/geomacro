**Target:** 193 UN members plus PSE and VAT, in Geopolitics, Macro/FX, and Critical Minerals, = 585 distinct cells. The owner has requested that genuine live, independently verified commercial coverage be proved **before** expanding other products.

## What the running evidence actually supports

The repository already runs six full-continent bounded original-source sweeps every two hours and retains real source results, plus a daily 585-cell rollup. An official source reply, 585 configured route cells, a journal headline, a macro **reference year**, or three shared signed hot products is *not* evidence of a fresh original item for every country-category.

The public 195-source rollup has source identity, native-date first-party page evidence and independent **source family** indicators, but explicitly does not have independent same-event confirmation, per-original legal rights, individual Ed25519 reviewer signatures, B2/D1 customer publication or x402 settlement acceptance. The existing private `auditTrustedCountryEventEvidence` verifies those signed source reviews, not 3/3 hot publication.

## Newly enforced reconciliation

`scripts/lib/country-195-commercial-original-review-join.mjs` uses the same exact 195-country registry and combines actual 585 country-source checkpoints with **the real existing independent same-event + rights Ed25519 verifier** (not arbitrary `verified:true` JSON flags). It tracks each of the 585 exact country-category keys, genuine fresh first-party originals, cryptographically qualified reviewed evidence and the subset where BOTH are available. It never upgrades either one to a signed commercial customer product without a separate outside-in B2/D1 serving contract.

The daily read-only `Country 195 Rolling Original Proof Audit` now runs a safe **prepublication** 585-cell reconciliation and emits a short-lived `country-195x3-prepublication-review-gaps-<run>` receipt. That scheduled public job has no access to private reviewer event packages; its review counter must therefore be interpreted as **NOT ASSESSED IN THIS JOB**, not a factual assertion that no rights exist in the world. It performs no extra network provider requests, no Supabase, no B2 writes, no source-data exposure and no payment. Existing private signed reviewer packages can separately be passed to this *same* pure function in a private scoped runner, where the original Ed25519 verifier is applied.

**Acceptance must fail closed:** not even a 585/585 original-source snapshot plus 585/585 signed reviews is by itself 585/585 commercial delivery. Every alleged paid country-category must independently show a real customer deliverable, legal source rights, exact current GRO identity/signature, B2 immutable full readback, D1 hot scope, published API response and stable pricing. No evidence supplied = `NOT_VERIFIED`, no charge.

## Live vs real-time clarification

Verified *capability to answer* in 195 countries is a different SLA from *new relevant original news published by independent organizations in all 195 × 3 cells within 24 hours*. The latter is **not achievable by promise or software configuration**; some countries do not publish a new qualifying minerals/macroeconomic/geopolitical event daily. The correct outcome on an otherwise healthy response may be `NO_CURRENT_QUALIFYING_EVENT`, alongside an independently verified **on-demand source reachability** test. Never fabricate an event or relabel a 2024/2025 statistical period as October 2026.
