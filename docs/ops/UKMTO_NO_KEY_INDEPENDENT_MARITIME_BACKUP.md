# UKMTO denied: independent no-key geopolitical maritime context

The legacy UKMTO endpoint is intentionally not retried from this fallback. The
three independent original-publisher routes (not republished UKMTO warnings):

- MARAD-hosted U.S. Maritime Alert and Advisory System,
  `https://www.maritime.dot.gov/msci-advisories`. It is a **multi-agency U.S.
  Government advisory**, not uniquely authored by MARAD. A public ACTIVE
  advisory effective date does not assert a new maritime incident on that date.
- ReCAAP Information Sharing Centre (Asia),
  `https://www.recaap.org/reports`. The periodic weekly-report index is
  historical/regional context and its coverage date range is not the article
  publication instant or a real-time attack.
- European Union Naval Force MSCIO,
  `https://www.mscio.eu/alerts/`. If the publisher responds with a
  client-rendered `Recent Incidents (0)` shell, the no-browser observer records
  exactly that shell: **zero individually verified incidents**. An independently
  pinned original alert readback and actual original publication clock are
  required for an event. No fake follow-link construction or scraper bypass.

Hosted by the already-scheduled `expanded-official-source-observation.yml`
job `independent-maritime-free-context`. Exactly 3 first-party no-key read-only
requests per run; bounded body/MIME/HTTPS host, no redirects, 8-second timeout;
single safe ephemeral aggregate JSON artifact, no copied reports or private
upstream article URL exposure, no extra cron, no B2/D1/Supabase writes.

**Scope:** maritime geopolitical trade-corridor research context only. Macro/FX
or Critical Minerals supply-chain risk may cite *an independently reviewed
event* when relevant, but a source catalogue alone never produces 195×3 live
country certification.

The publisher's actual website copyright and commercial reuse terms for each
particular report must be independently verified before commercial extraction
or signed customer evidence. Free to read != free to resell. This change
intentionally produces no signed GRO, no Facts/Rights approval, no numerical
GRI, no paid x402 quote and no 3/3 product health pass.

**True acceptance:** per-source transport and index shape receipts, optional
independent original alert readback if the publisher serves one with correct
time; genuine source-specific rights and independent signatures for downstream
paid products. If UKMTO remains denied, do not mark it ACTIVE or infer an
incident from a MARAD advisory or ReCAAP weekly report.
