learn the country's ISO3 code. It now imports the canonical 193 UN members +
VAT/PSE registry and builds all 585 country-category cases, plus a bounded
195-country directional corridor ring, under the existing engine. Territories
are not substituted for baseline countries. The old two-country macro/mineral
spot checks are no longer advertised as full three-category global coverage.

provider sweep is launched into a known outage. When healthy, at most 32
availability requests cover the cases; new requests are paced below unchanged
burst and per-client limits. A denial, malformed plan/proof, wrong price or
network failure stops immediately without retry. Failed sanitized artifacts
are retained. The existing six-hour schedule is unchanged.

paid resource. Unavailable inputs advertise no price; deliverable but unbound
or legally unqualified inputs remain no-charge. The checker validates exact
plan/subjects/modules and the founder-locked catalogue rather than assuming a
flat 0.05 USDC. It never sends payment or authorization headers.

original evidence or a paid customer payload readback. The report explicitly
keeps those checks unperformed. Global all-green closure additionally requires:

4. Deployed website transport to D1 plus current signed products and actual
   customer-delivery verification. No actual settlement is needed for the
