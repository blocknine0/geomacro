# GeoMacro paid structured intelligence: source intake and commercial output (29 Oct 2026)

Founder-defined product decision:

1. **Source intake is broad and private.** All private OSINT, official sources, market/economic releases and other news sources may enter the existing internal research pipeline as candidates without individual source-by-source commercial-use preclearance to *collect and analyze*. This policy does not change upstream crawler behavior, privacy, robots/access controls, or source recording.
2. **GeoMacro-owned transformation, not raw resale.** Transform canonical already-held evidence into original derived structured assertions: scope, verified observation, uncertainty, conditional market-impact channels, what to watch, comparison state, and refresh guidance. No raw text, article bytes, third-party URLs/IDs, upstream private names or unlicensed republishes in buyer output.
3. **Commercial reuse is distinct from collection.** Do **not** treat original phrasing as automatically granting a new commercial right. Existing per-output commercial use/permission and verified factual status gates remain for paid publication, especially for explicitly restricted sources. Founder acceptance of potential legal responsibility does not itself create a licence.
4. **Same truth and payment engine.** PREDGE, canonical D1 and B2 remain untouched; paid user/machine queries interpret one question into the approved SKU, call the exact existing canonical x402 request, return either a no-charge refusal, 402 payment challenge, or immutable paid JSON after authenticated settlement.
5. **Retain signed GRO integrity.** Only verified, unexpired existing GeoMacro Ed25519 signed canonical GRO can be returned as a signed product. Source envelopes, internal triage signatures and single unsigned OSINT events are *not* GRO signatures. Add the original signature bytes and signing-key identifier to newly prepared signed customer attestation; verify exact cryptographic object separately.
6. **Risk Gate stays Coming Soon.** All three approved prices remain discoverable; no new public checkout. Old frozen paid delivery replay remains unchanged.
7. **User and machine usefulness.** All nine launch-scope variants include a source-free `decision_intelligence[*].market_watch` on newly prepared output, with scenario channels selected exclusively from already-qualified evidence, fixed per-tier limits, state-version pointer and bounded refresh hint. No invented spot price, numeric forecast or "guaranteed market direction".
8. **No guaranteed immediate paid acceptance.** Deterministic interpretation is in-process, but canonical evidence availability, signer configuration, settlement/verification, Cloudflare quota and deployment may still delay or refuse a purchase. Do not claim 100% availability.

## Contract map

- Country/corridor digest: one verified dimension, at most one market-watch channel.
- Country/corridor profile: multiple verified dimensions, up to three market-watch channels.
- Intelligence focused/standard/complex: 2 / 3 / 5 conditional channels maximum, if evidence supports them.
- Signed GRO existing/new: up to two conditional channels, only from qualified risk state/driver or qualified structural observations; actual genuine GRO signature required.
- Risk Gate: Coming Soon, not activated.

## Customer journey

`POST /api/v1/intelligence/structured-answer` (question + optional subject/product): source-free planner -> exact canonical query hash -> governed availability -> prepared immutable structured output -> fixed USDC quote -> 402 -> buyer-supplied PAYMENT-SIGNATURE -> existing on-chain verification/settlement -> frozen 200 JSON. MCP `paid_structured_answer` exposes the same machinery. No customer-supplied price, source, signing material or payment override.

- Validate external URL censorship, source confidentiality, denied/blocked rights paths and empty/stale state refusal.
