# Risk Indices numerical continuity: certified parent and public Edge health

This applies to the **existing** 30-minute verified-parent repair job. It does not modify the public Risk Indices page, PREDGE, historical repository, payment paths or source-verification rules.

- The already-running GRI signed-ready backstop checks fresh immutable pointers every 15 minutes. They are notifications only. No one may manufacture reviewer signatures or country/category certificates from signed news, internal triage scores, SHA-shaped metadata or consent.
- The verified-parent backstop still performs exact-main source checks, idempotent dispatch and at most one bounded retry, now with strict three-category numerical child checks after #3056.
- At the end of the **same** existing 30-minute backstop, a no-funds observer distinguishes:
  1. `CURRENT_SIGNED_THREE_INDICES_VERIFIED`: independent signed parent/three-domain D1-and-Edge native timestamp/proof readback succeeded.
  2. `CURRENT_SIGNED_PARENT_INDICES_DELIVERY_NOT_VERIFIED`: a currently certified GRI exists but the public Risk Indices chain is broken. The job fails visibly and retains the existing protected repair flow.
  3. `NO_CURRENT_SIGNED_GRI_PARENT`: no real authenticated current parent. This is an external reviewer/certification dependency, **not a successful numerical launch**. Wait for actual independently approved current input and certificates.
  4. `PARENT_HTTP_UNAVAILABLE`: cannot independently establish canonical parent due to source HTTP failure. Distinct from missing external review.
- The observer never reads source URLs, raw articles, signing private keys, B2 original objects or Supabase. It only performs public D1/Edge bounded reads and reports existence/verification booleans. It prints no private pointer SHA. No new cron or additional cloud storage.
- Required production acceptance: existing scheduled job actually executes on canonical main; observe the true status, independent signed publisher from genuine reviewed data, exact D1→Edge proof; 72h sustained delivery monitoring before claiming uninterrupted operation.

**Reality boundary:** One-time owner consent grants conditional owner approval, not an independent Suman numerical or rights certificate. Automated triage SHA signatures are not substitutes. Complete, indefinitely continuous *certified current* index publication needs continuous generation of genuine independently authorized current signed reviewer inputs and country/category certificates. The runtime cannot honestly invent these.
