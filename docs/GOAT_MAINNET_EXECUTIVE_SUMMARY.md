# Geomacro × GOAT — Executive Summary

Geomacro is verified geopolitical and macro risk intelligence infrastructure for human and machine decisions. It converts live risk context into explainable scores, structured Risk Objects and Risk Gate decision context that can be consumed by financial teams and autonomous software systems.

## Why GOAT

GOAT x402 gives autonomous agents a machine-native payment rail for buying external intelligence on demand. The Geomacro use case is straightforward: an agent requests a bounded risk preflight, receives an HTTP 402 payment challenge, follows its own price/spend policy, and receives structured intelligence only after server-side merchant verification and exact order reconciliation.

This is not a prediction-market use case and not a synthetic token-transfer demo. The product being purchased is machine-readable geopolitical and macro risk intelligence.
