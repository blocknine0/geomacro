# Founding Pilot Checklist

Before a founding-pilot conversation:

- confirm current deployed commit;
- verify primary intelligence and GRI surfaces;
- keep Risk Gate labelled Private Pilot;
- share `/demo` only by direct link when technically relevant;
- state `execution_authorized=false`;
- define one buyer, one workflow and one geographic/corridor scope;
- agree success criteria and limitations;
- do not represent x402 testnet pricing as commercial pricing;
- preserve evidence from the conversation and pilot before expanding scope.
