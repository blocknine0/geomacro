## What Geomacro is now

Geomacro is geopolitical and macro risk intelligence for operational decisions. It turns current events and governed macro data into explainable risk context, signed machine-readable Risk Objects, and Risk Gate recommendations before a human or software system takes an action.

The commercial product is not the prediction-market layer. Arc, Circle, USDC, x402 and earlier prediction-market work are supporting technical proof for programmable delivery and agent integration.

## The buyer problem

Teams making cross-border, country-exposure, treasury, supplier, market-entry or automated financial decisions often have geopolitical and macro information, but not a compact decision-ready object with:

- current risk score and direction;
- evidence and provenance;
- confidence and freshness;
- commercial/source-rights status;
- cryptographic integrity;
- a policy-aware recommendation that can fail closed.

Geomacro is intended to sit between raw information and the buyer-controlled decision or execution layer.

- all-country support until the production country census proves the exact accepted set;
