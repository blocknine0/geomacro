# Public Demo Boundary

The `/demo` implementation is a controlled technical-demo surface. It is not listed in the public sitemap and crawler access is blocked through `public/robots.txt` while Early Access remains controlled.

A direct demo link may be shared selectively with technical partners or pilot prospects after confirming the deployed build and current staging/production configuration. The route must continue to state that the browser pre-flight does not move funds, structural evidence is separate from GRI v1.2, corridor scope is endpoint-composed, and x402 uses Arc Testnet technical-proof pricing rather than institutional pricing.

Do not add `/demo` to the sitemap or promote it as a general production service without a deliberate launch decision and a fresh deployment/readiness review.
