# 8. What Data Geomacro Can Use

Geomacro documents intelligence categories and governance rules rather than presenting a raw provider roster as the product.

## 8.1 Macro intelligence

Potential structured inputs include inflation, real GDP growth, unemployment, debt and fiscal stress, trade dependence, external balance, monetary conditions, currency stress and growth trends.

## 8.2 Geopolitical intelligence

Potential structured inputs include conflict pressure, sanctions exposure, political instability, displacement, border escalation, diplomatic stress, security incidents and state-fragility signals.

## 8.3 Strategic resources

Potential structured inputs include mineral production and reserves, refining capacity, import/export dependence, supply-chain concentration, export restrictions and strategic chokepoints.

## 8.4 Trade and corridors

Potential structured inputs include bilateral trade exposure, concentration, tariffs, export restrictions, shipping disruption, chokepoints and corridor dependence.

## 8.5 Energy and system shocks

Potential structured inputs include oil/gas exposure, electricity vulnerability, infrastructure disruption, natural disasters and other material system shocks where suitable data and usage rights exist.

## 8.6 Live event intelligence

Structured event fields can include category, affected geography, timestamps, severity, confidence, source identity, story grouping, freshness and classification provenance.

> Availability varies by dataset, geography, time period and product policy. A possible field is not a promise that a complete production dataset is currently available for every country or date.
