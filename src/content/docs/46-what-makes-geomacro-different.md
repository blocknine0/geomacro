# 46. What Makes Geomacro Different?

Geomacro is designed to turn geopolitical, macroeconomic and critical-mineral developments into explainable decision context rather than stop at a headline summary.

Its differentiating architecture combines:

1. **Structured risk intelligence** — events become comparable machine-readable signals.
2. **Transparent Risk Indices** — Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals risk are presented separately with current readings, history, evidence, confidence and audited GRI v1.2 proof lineage.
3. **Change attribution** — score movement is decomposed rather than presented as an unexplained number.
4. **Verifiable Risk Objects** — subject context carries versioning, freshness, hashes and issuer signatures.
5. **Pre-flight policy separation** — Risk Gate evaluates external context while customer identity, permissions, policy and execution remain customer-controlled.
6. **Human + machine access from one core** — Free Explorer, professional workflows, API/Risk Gate and pre-launch pay-per-call agent access sit around the same governed intelligence architecture rather than creating separate data truths.
7. **Explicit commercial boundaries** — source rights, entitlement, freshness and deliverability determine what can be sold; payment success cannot widen the product or authorize a transaction.

The product is not positioned as a generic chatbot, raw news feed, autonomous trading system or prediction-market company.
