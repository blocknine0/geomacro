# 46. What Makes Geomacro Different?

Geomacro is designed to turn geopolitical and macro developments into explainable decision context rather than stop at a headline summary.

Its differentiating architecture combines:

1. **Structured risk intelligence** — events become comparable machine-readable signals.
2. **Transparent aggregation** — GRI exposes methodology, coverage, confidence, concentration controls and proof.
3. **Change attribution** — score movement is decomposed rather than presented as an unexplained number.
4. **Verifiable Risk Objects** — subject context carries versioning, freshness, hashes and issuer signatures.
5. **Pre-flight policy separation** — Risk Gate evaluates external context while customer execution remains customer-controlled.
6. **Human + machine surfaces** — the same intelligence fabric supports public research, Ask Geomacro and machine APIs without creating separate data truths.

The product is not positioned as a generic chatbot, raw news feed or prediction-market company.
