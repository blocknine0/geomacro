# 24. Access Levels: Public to Institutional

Geomacro separates what is publicly available today from what is being offered through controlled Private Pilot access and what remains planned.

## Public — LIVE

Current public surfaces include:

- risk intelligence
- Global Risk Index and verification context
- Ask Geomacro
- public research and methodology
- technical-proof surfaces clearly labelled as Testnet/secondary

## Private Pilot

Controlled machine/institutional access currently includes the Risk API and Risk Gate foundation for approved pilot workflows.

Private Pilot does **not** imply a generally available production SLA, automated enterprise onboarding or fully validated institutional methodology.

## Professional / Team — PLANNED COMMERCIAL DIRECTION

Potential future capabilities include deeper history, alerts, exports, advanced Ask workflows, saved monitoring and professional workspaces.

## Institutional / Enterprise — PLANNED + PRIVATE PILOT PATH

Potential capabilities include structured API access, Risk Objects, Risk Gate, audit/provenance tooling, monitoring, team permissions, custom integrations and support commitments where they can actually be sustained.

Exact pricing, quotas and service commitments are not yet publicly final.
