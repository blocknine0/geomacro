# 24. Access Levels: Public to Institutional

Geomacro separates what is publicly available today from controlled Private Pilot access and broader institutional expansion.

## Public — LIVE

Current public surfaces include:

- Risk Intelligence
- separate Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals Risk Indices with verification context
- Ask Geomacro
- public research and methodology
- technical-proof surfaces clearly labelled as secondary and non-authorizing

The public Risk Indices preserve the versioned GRI v1.2 parent methodology and verified proof lineage. Historical combined-GRI snapshots remain versioned audit records rather than a second live headline product.

## Private Pilot

Controlled machine/institutional access currently includes the Risk API, signed country/directional-corridor Risk Objects and Risk Gate foundation for approved workflows.

Private Pilot does **not** imply an unrestricted production SLA, automated enterprise onboarding, transaction authorization or a guarantee that every request shape is deliverable. `execution_authorized=false` remains the Geomacro Risk Gate boundary.

## Professional / Team — CONTROLLED COMMERCIAL DIRECTION

Supported packaging can include deeper history, alerts, exports, advanced Ask workflows, saved monitoring and professional workspaces as those capabilities are enabled for the agreed scope.

## Institutional / Enterprise — CONTROLLED EXPANSION

Institutional capabilities can include structured API access, Risk Objects, Risk Gate, audit/provenance tooling, monitoring, team permissions, custom integrations and support commitments where they can actually be sustained.

Exact pricing, quotas and service commitments are defined by the live product contract and customer agreement rather than inferred from public documentation.
