# 38. Global Coverage

Geomacro is designed as global geopolitical and macro risk intelligence, but different product layers have different coverage requirements.

## Public global layer

The Global Risk Index is an aggregate over qualifying evidence in its current three scoring domains. Its coverage field discloses active-domain availability; missing domains are excluded rather than converted into zero risk.

## Subject-specific layer

Country and corridor Risk Objects are currently a **Private Pilot** product wedge. Coverage should be described only for subjects and inputs actually supported by the pilot data path.

## Historical layer

The separate historical-data infrastructure broadens reproducible source coverage across geopolitics, macro and strategic-resource datasets. Historical ingestion does not automatically make every dataset commercially eligible or suitable for live decision delivery.

"Global" therefore describes the intended intelligence scope, not a claim that every country, corridor, event family and data dimension has identical live coverage.
