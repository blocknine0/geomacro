# 10. Source Privacy

Geomacro's customer product is structured risk intelligence, not a raw internal source directory.

Customer-facing evidence and proof can include information such as:

- evidence count
- source count
- independent-story count
- confidence and freshness
- source or evidence references where permitted
- provenance availability
- methodology and proof versions
- calculation, input, evidence and methodology hashes
- exact change attribution where available

Internal provider configuration, credentials, commercial terms and source-policy implementation details need not be exposed publicly.

Source privacy does not mean unverifiable output: Geomacro's goal is to expose enough evidence, attribution and cryptographic/provenance detail to inspect a published risk result without leaking credentials or restricted source material.
