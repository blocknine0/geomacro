# 47. Core Product Principle

> **One shared intelligence architecture. Multiple product-specific access and decision policies.**

Risk Intelligence, the separate Risk Indices, Ask Geomacro, Professional access, Risk API, Risk Gate, Research and agent/institutional interfaces should not maintain conflicting copies of reality.

They can differ in:

- scope
- admission policy
- access and entitlement
- depth and history
- customer policy
- output format
- commercial rights
- pricing/payment rail
- presentation

but should preserve common provenance and versioned truth wherever they consume the same underlying intelligence.

A payment rail, subscription or marketplace listing is therefore a delivery mechanism around the intelligence core. It must not create a parallel risk engine, widen source rights or grant downstream execution authority.

The second principle is equally important:

> **Explain the concept first, professional detail second, technical proof third.**

This keeps Geomacro understandable to traders, risk teams, finance professionals and operators while preserving the technical proof needed by developers and due-diligence reviewers.
