# 47. Core Product Principle

> **One shared intelligence architecture. Multiple product-specific decision policies.**

GRI, Ask Geomacro, Risk API, Risk Gate, Research and future institutional/agent interfaces should not maintain conflicting copies of reality.

They can differ in:

- scope
- admission policy
- access and entitlement
- depth and history
- customer policy
- output format
- commercial rights
- presentation

but should preserve common provenance and versioned truth wherever they consume the same underlying intelligence.

The second principle is equally important:

> **Explain the concept first, professional detail second, technical proof third.**

This keeps Geomacro understandable to risk teams, finance professionals and operators without hiding the technical proof needed by developers or due-diligence reviewers.
