The current public Risk Indices preserve the versioned GRI v1.2 parent methodology and verified proof lineage. Historical combined-GRI snapshots remain versioned audit records rather than a second current headline product.

Geomacro maintains controlled pricing, support, incident and customer-use boundaries for commercial and Private Pilot delivery. Public documentation is not a production uptime/latency SLA and is not evidence of a customer relationship. Customer-specific quotas and contractual service commitments are agreed only through an appropriate SOW/agreement and only where current operational evidence supports them.

The commercial model prioritizes recurring professional intelligence, institutional/API delivery and agent-native machine access, while programmable-finance applications remain secondary to the intelligence product unless a live runtime explicitly advertises an active commercial rail.

Private Pilot Risk Gate remains non-authorizing with `execution_authorized=false`; customer identity, permissions, policy, funds and downstream execution remain customer-controlled.

Contact `contact@geomacro.live` for a controlled commercial or institutional conversation.
