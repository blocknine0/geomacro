## Commercial access ladder

### Free Explorer

Public website access includes Risk Intelligence, the three Risk Indices, Ask Geomacro, public research and methodology. It is not a free structured API.

### Pay per call

The prepared production machine product is `geomacro_adaptive_risk_intelligence_v1`.

- prepared price: **0.02 USDC per successful paid call**;
- deliverability is checked before payment;
- unsupported or unavailable requests are not charged;
- the live HTTP 402 challenge or approved provider plan is authoritative when production is enabled;
- production real-money activation remains disabled until coordinated launch gates and explicit owner authorization are complete.

### Professional intelligence

The controlled founding package starts with **5,000 credits / 30 days** and can include deeper analytics, historical context, attribution, governed views and agreed exports.

### API + Risk Gate

The controlled founding machine package starts with **20,000 credits / 30 days** and can include authenticated governed data, country/corridor profiles, signed Risk Objects and Risk Gate bundles for an agreed workflow.

### Institutional

The current institutional starting model uses a **100,000-credit monthly starting pool**, followed by contracted volume, coverage, controls and support scope where implemented.

These packages are not evidence of general availability, paying-customer traction, a universal production SLA or unrestricted data rights. Customer-specific quotas, use rights and contractual commitments remain subject to the appropriate agreement and current operational evidence.

## Execution boundary

Risk Gate remains non-authorizing with `execution_authorized=false`. Customer identity, permissions, policy, funds and downstream execution remain customer-controlled.

Payment success purchases the bounded intelligence product. It does not authorize a trade, payment, transfer or other customer action.

## Technical-proof boundary

Prediction markets remain Testnet-only. Arc, Circle/CCTP, Bridge & Swap and related onchain surfaces remain secondary Technical Proof unless a page explicitly states otherwise. A prepared mainnet machine-payment adapter does not turn those technical-proof surfaces into the commercial product.
