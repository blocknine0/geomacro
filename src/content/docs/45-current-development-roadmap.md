# 45. Current Development Roadmap

Geomacro's expansion order is deliberately evidence-led so public claims follow implemented and observed operating state.

## Current production work

- keep website, repository and documentation aligned as one source of truth
- maintain Risk Indices/data reliability, versioned GRI v1.2 proof lineage and provenance controls
- harden Risk Object / Risk Gate contracts
- maintain security, privacy and resilience evidence
- verify commercial source eligibility
- preserve fail-closed payment and entitlement boundaries

## Controlled expansion

- staging and production HTTP/DB load evidence
- signing-key rotation/recovery drills
- security remediation and re-test where findings exist
- Private Pilot package and pricing
- design-partner validation
- customer-specific operating terms and quotas

## Scale-out

- permanent commercial demonstrations where they remain useful
- reusable buyer and partner materials
- paid and recurring commercial validation
- broader governed production integrations where justified

Production expansion remains downstream of product, repository, website, security and runtime alignment so customer-facing material does not become stale or overstate capability.
