# 32. Strategic Resources and Supply Chain

Strategic-resource intelligence is part of Geomacro's current data architecture.

Rare earth / critical-mineral risk is also one of the **three current GRI v1.2 scoring domains**, alongside geopolitics and macro.

Current GRI use should not be confused with a complete supply-chain product. A future dedicated surface could add:

- production and reserve concentration
- refining concentration
- import/export dependence
- export restrictions
- strategic commodity exposure
- corridor/chokepoint context
- supplier and industrial dependency graphs

Those richer supply-chain workflows are **PLANNED** unless separately implemented.

Source licensing and commercial-use eligibility remain explicit gates for any paid strategic-resource dataset or derived output.
