# 51. Summary

Geomacro is geopolitical and macro risk intelligence infrastructure for human and machine decisions.

```text
world events + structured data
        ↓
evidence + provenance
        ↓
structured risk intelligence
        ↓
subject Risk Objects (Private Pilot)
        ↓
attribution + confidence + freshness + integrity
        ↓
Ask Geomacro / Risk API / Risk Gate / Research
        ↓
human or customer-controlled machine decision
```

The current public product includes Risk Intelligence and Ask Geomacro.

The Risk Indices website page is retired. Historical proofs remain archived as audit evidence.

Country/corridor Risk Objects and Risk Gate are **Private Pilot** infrastructure.


Geomacro's commercial thesis is to make geopolitical, macroeconomic and critical-mineral risk explainable, reproducible and usable by professionals, APIs and autonomous systems before consequential actions are taken, while leaving authorization and execution under customer control.
