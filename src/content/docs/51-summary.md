# 51. Summary

Geomacro is geopolitical and macro risk intelligence infrastructure for human and machine decisions.

```text
world events + structured data
        ↓
evidence + provenance
        ↓
structured risk intelligence
        ↓
Global Risk Index / subject Risk Objects
        ↓
attribution + confidence + freshness + integrity
        ↓
Ask Geomacro / Risk API / Risk Gate / Research
        ↓
human or customer-controlled machine decision
```

The current public product includes risk intelligence, the verified Global Risk Index and Ask Geomacro.

Country/corridor Risk Objects and Risk Gate are **Private Pilot** infrastructure.

Prediction markets, Arc Testnet, USDC/Circle/CCTP and Bridge & Swap remain valuable **Technical Proof**, but they are not the primary company identity.

Geomacro's commercial thesis is to make geopolitical and macro risk explainable, auditable and usable by professionals, APIs and autonomous systems before consequential actions are taken.
