# 51. Summary

Geomacro is geopolitical, macroeconomic and critical-mineral risk intelligence infrastructure for human and machine decisions.

```text
world events + structured data
        ↓
evidence + provenance
        ↓
structured risk intelligence
        ↓
separate Risk Indices / subject Risk Objects
        ↓
attribution + confidence + freshness + integrity
        ↓
Free Explorer / Ask / Professional / API / Risk Gate / agent access
        ↓
human or customer-controlled machine decision
```

The current public product includes Risk Intelligence, separate Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals Risk Indices, Ask Geomacro and Free Explorer.

The public indices preserve the audited GRI v1.2 parent methodology and proof lineage. Historical combined-GRI snapshots remain versioned audit records rather than a second live headline score.

Geomacro's commercial access ladder is:

1. **Free Explorer — LIVE**
2. **Pay per call — MAINNET PRE-LAUNCH**
3. **Professional intelligence — FOUNDING PILOT**
4. **API + Risk Gate — PRIVATE / FOUNDING PILOT**
5. **Institutional — CONTRACTED / PILOT-LED**

The prepared pay-per-call path uses a 0.02 USDC per successful paid call launch price, while the live HTTP 402 challenge or approved provider plan remains authoritative when production is enabled. Real-money activation is disabled until coordinated launch gates and explicit owner authorization are complete.

Country/corridor Risk Objects and Risk Gate are **Private Pilot** infrastructure. Risk Gate remains non-authorizing with `execution_authorized=false`; customer identity, permissions, policy, funds and downstream execution remain customer-controlled.

Prediction markets, Arc Testnet, USDC/Circle/CCTP and Bridge & Swap remain valuable **Technical Proof**, but they are not the primary company identity. Prediction markets remain permanently Testnet-only.

Geomacro's commercial thesis is to make geopolitical, macroeconomic and critical-mineral risk explainable, auditable and usable by professionals, APIs and autonomous systems before consequential actions are taken, without turning risk intelligence into delegated transaction authority.
