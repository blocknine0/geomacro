# 28. Partner Architecture

**Status: DESIGN-PARTNER / PRIVATE PILOT DIRECTION**

Geomacro's first partner motion should validate a narrow real workflow before broad enterprise packaging.

Potential partner profiles include:

- treasury and cross-border payment teams
- institutional risk and strategy teams
- financial infrastructure providers
- AI-agent builders
- critical-mineral and supply-chain operators

A partner can consume the same canonical intelligence through a scoped product policy rather than receiving a separate data truth.

```text
Geomacro intelligence core
        ↓
verified subject context
        ↓
partner entitlement / policy
        ↓
API, Risk Gate, report or monitored workflow
```

Partner redistribution rights remain separate from underlying source rights.

Geomacro should not claim deployed institutional partners, production SLAs or customer adoption unless those relationships and commitments actually exist.
