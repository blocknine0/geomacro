# 33. Historical Intelligence

**Status: DATA INFRASTRUCTURE ACTIVE / COMMERCIAL PACKAGING PLANNED**

Geomacro maintains a separate historical-data repository for reproducible geopolitical, macro, rare-earth and other source observations.

The historical layer supports work such as:

- source-governed backfill
- long-range trend research
- methodology evaluation
- deterministic replay
- event and country history
- validation and benchmarking

Historical ingestion is separate from the live publication path. Backfilled data must not be allowed to rewrite what Geomacro could have known at an earlier live snapshot.

Commercial historical API/dataset packaging is not yet generally available. Source rights, coverage and validation must be reviewed before paid redistribution or delivery.
