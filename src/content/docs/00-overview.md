# Geomacro Documentation

Geomacro is geopolitical, macroeconomic and critical-mineral risk intelligence infrastructure for human and machine decisions.

> **Current status:** Public Risk Intelligence, the separate Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals Risk Indices, Ask Geomacro and Free Explorer are **LIVE**. Professional intelligence is **FOUNDING PILOT**. Governed API delivery, signed country/directional-corridor Risk Objects and Risk Gate are **PRIVATE PILOT**. AI-agent pay per call is **MAINNET PRE-LAUNCH** with real-money activation disabled. Arc, Circle/CCTP, Bridge & Swap and prediction-market functionality remain **TECHNICAL PROOF / secondary layers**, and prediction markets remain Testnet-only.

This documentation separates implemented capability from controlled pilot access, pre-launch production preparation, technical proof and planned work. It does not imply paying-customer traction, an independent external security audit, a production SLA or institutional deployment unless explicitly stated.

## Product hierarchy

1. Risk Intelligence
2. Risk Indices: Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals
3. Ask Geomacro
4. Access & Pricing: Free Explorer, pay per call, Professional, API + Risk Gate, Institutional
5. Risk Objects and Risk Gate
6. Data / API and institutional workflows
7. Research and methodology
8. Secondary technical-proof layers

The current public Risk Indices preserve the audited **gri-v1.2.0** parent methodology and proof lineage. The historical combined GRI remains a versioned audit reference, not a second live headline score. Geomacro's Risk Indices are intelligence signals, not market probabilities.

The commercial access model does not create separate risk engines. Human subscriptions, API entitlements and machine-payment rails all sit around the same governed intelligence core and cannot widen source rights or execution authority.
