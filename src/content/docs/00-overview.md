# Geomacro Documentation

Geomacro is geopolitical and macro risk intelligence infrastructure for human and machine decisions.

> **Current status:** Public Risk Intelligence, the separate Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals Risk Indices, and grounded Ask Geomacro are live product surfaces. Risk Gate and signed machine-delivery interfaces are **Private Pilot**. Paid machine delivery is production-gated and remains fail-closed until explicitly activated.

This documentation separates implemented capability from Private Pilot, production-gated commercial access and planned work. It does not imply an independent external security audit, production SLA or institutional deployment unless explicitly stated.

---

## Product hierarchy

1. Risk Intelligence
2. Risk Indices: Geopolitical, Macroeconomic and Critical Minerals
3. Ask Geomacro
4. Risk Objects and Risk Gate
5. Data / API and institutional workflows
6. Research and methodology

The current public Risk Indices preserve the audited **gri-v1.2.0** parent methodology and proof lineage. The historical combined GRI remains a versioned audit reference, not a second live headline score. Geomacro's risk indices are intelligence signals, not market probabilities.
