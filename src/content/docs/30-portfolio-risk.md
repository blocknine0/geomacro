# 30. Portfolio Risk

**Status: PLANNED**

Portfolio risk is a future applied-intelligence surface that could combine customer-defined exposures with Geomacro's country, event and macro context.

Potential outputs include:

- exposure-weighted external risk
- concentration by country or region
- largest risk contributors
- fastest-changing exposures
- scenario or event sensitivity
- evidence and confidence context

A portfolio implementation would require customer-supplied exposure data and explicit product methodology. No live institutional portfolio-risk product should be inferred from this roadmap page.
