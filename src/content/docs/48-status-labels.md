# 48. Status Labels

Geomacro uses a small, explicit status vocabulary across public surfaces so visitors can distinguish deployed capability from controlled access, pre-launch preparation and technical proof.

## LIVE

Available in the current public product.

Examples: Risk Intelligence, the three public Risk Indices, Ask Geomacro and Free Explorer.

## FOUNDING PILOT

Implemented commercial packaging available through controlled early customer access. It is not anonymous self-serve general availability and does not imply recurring-revenue traction, a production SLA or broad customer adoption.

## PRIVATE PILOT

Implemented capability available only through a scoped pilot or approved workflow, with product/operations still being hardened.

Examples include Risk Gate, signed country/directional-corridor Risk Objects and governed API delivery.

## MAINNET PRE-LAUNCH

A production payment or distribution path is code-prepared but **real-money activation remains disabled** until the required security, source-rights, operational, provider and coordinated-launch gates are complete and explicitly authorized.

Code existence, credentials or a prepared price do not make a PRE-LAUNCH product live.

## CONTRACTED / PILOT-LED

Availability depends on an agreed customer scope, entitlement, volume, controls and support boundary rather than a public self-serve plan.

## TECHNICAL PROOF

Working secondary implementation, often Testnet-based, that demonstrates engineering or integration capability but is not represented as the primary production commercial product.

Prediction markets remain permanently Testnet-only even when other machine-payment products later move to production.

## PLANNED

A roadmap or commercial direction that is not currently available.

## LEGACY

Older implementation retained for compatibility, reproducibility or technical evidence.

## Status rule

Status must describe the actual externally usable product state. Payment settlement, marketplace visibility or an internal implementation must not silently upgrade a product's public status.
