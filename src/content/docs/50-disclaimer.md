# 50. Disclaimer

Geomacro provides information and risk-intelligence decision-support tooling.

It does not provide financial, investment, legal, trading or sanctions-screening advice.

Risk scores, interpretations, Risk Gate recommendations and machine-readable responses are not guarantees of future outcomes and should be evaluated within the user's own policy, permissions, compliance and decision process.

Risk Gate does not custody funds or make the customer's final transaction decision. `execution_authorized=false` remains the Geomacro boundary in the current Private Pilot architecture.

A subscription, invoice or successful machine payment purchases only the bounded intelligence product described by the applicable entitlement. Payment does not authorize a trade, payment, transfer or other downstream customer action.

Where evidence is missing, stale, contradictory, commercially ineligible or unverifiable, Geomacro is designed to expose that limitation rather than manufacture certainty or accept payment for an undeliverable required result.

Mainnet pay-per-call access remains PRE-LAUNCH until real-money activation is deliberately authorized and verified. Testnet technical-proof functionality should not be interpreted as production real-money availability.
