# 29. Revenue Architecture

**Status: CONTROLLED COMMERCIALIZATION / PRE-REVENUE UNLESS AND UNTIL ACTUAL PAID REVENUE IS RECORDED**

Geomacro's revenue architecture is intelligence-first. Public intelligence remains useful without payment, while deeper professional workflows, governed machine delivery and contracted institutional use create the commercial path.

```text
Free Explorer · LIVE
      ↓
Professional intelligence · FOUNDING PILOT
      ↓
API + Risk Gate · PRIVATE / FOUNDING PILOT
      ↓
Institutional · CONTRACTED / PILOT-LED

AI-agent pay per call · MAINNET PRE-LAUNCH
      ↘ buys the same governed intelligence core per successful request
```

## Current commercial access model

### Free Explorer

Public Risk Intelligence, the three Risk Indices, Ask Geomacro, Research and methodology remain available for evaluation without a paid API entitlement.

### Professional intelligence

The controlled founding package starts at **5,000 credits / 30 days** and can include deeper history, analytics, attribution, governed country/corridor views and agreed exports.

### API + Risk Gate

The controlled founding machine package starts at **20,000 credits / 30 days** and can include authenticated governed data, signed Risk Objects and Risk Gate bundles for an agreed workflow.

### Institutional

The current starting model uses a **100,000-credit monthly pool**, followed by contracted volume, coverage, controls and support where implemented and supported by operational evidence.

### AI-agent pay per call

The prepared production machine product is `geomacro_adaptive_risk_intelligence_v1`.

- prepared price: **0.02 USDC per successful paid call**;
- deliverability is checked before payment;
- the live HTTP 402 challenge or approved provider plan is authoritative when production is enabled;
- production real-money activation is currently disabled pending coordinated launch gates and explicit owner authorization.

Pay per call is an access model, not a separate risk engine. It is intended for autonomous software that needs a bounded governed answer without first taking a monthly subscription.

## Potential recurring and contracted revenue surfaces

- professional intelligence subscriptions;
- advanced history, alerts and research workflows;
- structured data/API access;
- Risk Gate usage;
- enterprise monitoring and audit tooling;
- custom integrations;
- institutional contracts;
- embedded/partner distribution;
- approved agent-marketplace distribution.

Prediction-market and Arc Testnet flows are not the primary revenue thesis and prediction markets remain Testnet-only.

## Commercial controls

A subscription, invoice, machine payment or manual contract must resolve to a canonical entitlement. The entitlement determines what can be delivered. A payment rail does not widen source rights, subject coverage, historical depth, response limits or execution authority.

Customer-specific quotas, permitted use, support scope, security/data-handling obligations and legally binding terms belong in the applicable SOW/agreement.

Geomacro must not imply recurring revenue, paying customers, general availability, a production SLA or institutional adoption before those facts actually exist and are documented.
