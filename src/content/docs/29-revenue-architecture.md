# 29. Revenue Architecture

**Status: PRE-REVENUE COMMERCIALIZATION**

Geomacro's intended revenue architecture is intelligence-first.

```text
public intelligence
      ↓
professional intelligence
      ↓
Data / API
      ↓
institutional / enterprise workflows
      ↓
agent-native machine access
```

Potential revenue surfaces include:

- professional intelligence subscriptions
- advanced history, alerts and research workflows
- structured data/API access
- Risk Gate usage
- enterprise monitoring and audit tooling
- custom integrations
- institutional contracts
- embedded/partner distribution

Prediction-market and Arc Testnet flows are not the primary revenue thesis.

Geomacro now has an internal controlled founding-pilot standard for Early Access pricing, 30-day pilot structure, founder-support boundaries, incident handling and customer-use terms. These are founder-use commercial controls, not public list pricing, a production SLA, general availability or evidence of paying customers. Customer-specific quotas, scope and legally binding terms still belong in the signed SOW/agreement.

Geomacro must not imply recurring revenue or paying customers before they actually exist.
