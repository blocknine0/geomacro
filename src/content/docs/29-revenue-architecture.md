# 29. Revenue Architecture

**Status: COMMERCIAL DELIVERY ARCHITECTURE**

Geomacro's revenue architecture is intelligence-first.

```text
public intelligence
      ↓
professional intelligence
      ↓
Data / API
      ↓
institutional / enterprise workflows
      ↓
agent-native machine access
```

Commercial surfaces include:

- professional intelligence subscriptions
- advanced history, alerts and research workflows
- structured data/API access
- Risk Gate usage
- enterprise monitoring and audit tooling
- custom integrations
- institutional contracts
- embedded/partner distribution

Prediction-market and programmable-finance flows are secondary application layers, not the primary revenue thesis.

Geomacro maintains controlled commercial standards for pilot pricing, scoped access, founder-support boundaries, incident handling and customer-use terms. Customer-specific quotas, service scope and legally binding commitments belong in the signed SOW/agreement.

Static documentation never claims a payment rail, customer relationship or revenue event that is not supported by the corresponding live runtime or signed commercial record.
