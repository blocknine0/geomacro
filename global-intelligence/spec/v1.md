# Global Intelligence v1 Specification

## 1. Canonical coverage
Exactly 195 canonical country targets are represented by ISO2/ISO3 identifiers.

## 2. Category engines
### GEOPOLITICS
Political, security, conflict, diplomatic, maritime and geopolitical events.

### MACRO
Inflation, rates, monetary policy, fiscal policy, growth, employment, trade and macroeconomic events.

### CRITICAL_MINERALS
Critical minerals supply, production, processing, export controls, mine/refinery disruptions and strategic materials policy.

## 3. Source classes
- AUTHORITATIVE: official institutional source whose identity and provenance are verified.
- GLOBAL_FALLBACK: global baseline source used to maintain country coverage.
- SPECIALIST: credible domain-specific source used for corroboration.
- TELEGRAM_VERIFIED: Telegram source with verified institutional identity and approved evidence policy.
- TELEGRAM_EARLY_SIGNAL: useful low-latency signal that cannot independently establish a confirmed fact.

## 4. Verification states
- CONFIRMED
- CORROBORATED
- EARLY_SIGNAL
- CONFLICTING
- REJECTED

No unverified Telegram signal may be promoted to CONFIRMED solely because it is repeated by other unverified channels.

## 5. Output contract
Every answer carries category, country/entity scope, assessment, confirmed events, early signals, confidence, verification metadata, provenance, and update time.

## 6. Router
The router identifies one or more relevant categories before selecting category-specific evidence. Mixed questions may invoke multiple engines in parallel.

## 7. Certification
All 585 country/category cells must pass the v1 runtime gates before integration into production.
